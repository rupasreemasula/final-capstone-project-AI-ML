Q6 - JOIN: 10 highest-rated books with their category name
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SQL:
SELECT b.title, b.rating, b.price_gbp, c.category_name
        FROM books b
        JOIN categories c ON b.category_id = c.category_id
        WHERE b.rating >= 4
        ORDER BY b.rating DESC, b.title
        LIMIT 10;
